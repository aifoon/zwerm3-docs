# Quick start

This chapter takes you from a closed flight case to sound, in the shortest way. Every step has its own chapter further on.

## Before you start

You need:

- the bees, charged
- the router of the swarm, switched on
- a Mac with KweenB, connected to the router (by cable or over its Wi-Fi)

## Streaming from your audio software

1. **Switch on the router** and connect the Mac to its network.
2. **Switch on the bees.** A bee needs about half a minute before KweenB sees it.
3. **Open KweenB.** The **Swarm** page shows your bees. A bee that is on shows **Standby**.
4. **Choose the bees you use.** Click **1–4**, **1–8**, **1–12**, **1–16** or **All** next to **Use bees**, or drag single bees. See [Swarm](./04-swarm.md).
5. **Click Connect → Stream to the bees.** KweenB shows what it is doing and closes that window by itself when everything runs. The bees now show **Streaming**.
6. **In your audio software, choose "KweenB Swarm" as the output.** Each bee listens to one channel of that device. The **Routing** page shows which channel goes to which bee, and lets you change it. See [Routing](./06-routing.md).
7. **Play.**

![The Swarm page of KweenB](/img/manual/swarm.png)

## Triggering sounds that are on the bees

1. Do steps 1 to 4 above.
2. Click **Connect → Trigger only** (or **Stream to the bees**, if you also want to stream).
3. Open the **Audio** page and the **Trigger** tab.
4. Click a scene on the left, then **All** to play it on every bee that has it.

To trigger from a phone or tablet, see [Remote interface](./10-remote-interface.md). To trigger without the computer, see [Satellite](./11-satellite.md).

## When you are done

1. Click **Disconnect** in the top bar. The sound stops on all bees.
2. Click the **power button** at the top right and choose **Shutdown all bees**.
3. Wait a few seconds, then switch off the batteries of the bees.

:::tip
Keep an eye on the **Monitor** page during a show. It tells in plain words whether a bee needs you: a low battery, a weak Wi-Fi signal or sound that arrives too late.
:::
